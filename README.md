# Technical Capability Alignment Starter Kit

**A practical checklist and prompt for beginning a NIST and NICE Framework-aligned capability initiative**

This resource supports early discovery and alignment. It is not an official NIST or NICE compliance checklist. Adapt it to the organization, technical domain, and performance context.

## Before you begin: alignment checklist

### 1. Define the performance outcome

- What performance outcome is expected?
- Which business, operational, technology, or cybersecurity objective does it support?
- What does successful performance look like?
- Which performance standards apply: accuracy, timeliness, safety, documentation, escalation, or compliance?

### 2. Identify accountability

- Which role, team, or function is accountable for the outcome?
- Who performs the work?
- Who validates performance?
- Which stakeholders need to use the same capability language?

### 3. Identify the critical tasks

- What observable tasks demonstrate the capability?
- Which tasks are high-frequency, high-risk, or business-critical?
- What decisions must a person make while performing the task?
- What conditions, tools, systems, or constraints shape performance?

### 4. Map the required capability

- What knowledge supports the task?
- What skills enable successful performance?
- Which competencies or capability areas are relevant?
- For cybersecurity work, which NICE Framework Task, Knowledge, and Skill statements, Work Roles, or Competency Areas are relevant?

### 5. Connect the work to the operating context

- For cybersecurity work, which NIST Cybersecurity Framework function or outcome does the capability support?
- What internal standard, regulation, service expectation, or operating principle applies?
- What risk or opportunity does successful performance address?

### 6. Design the development path

- What level of proficiency is required?
- What learning experience best develops the capability: explanation, demonstration, guided practice, scenario, simulation, coaching, or performance support?
- Which educational structure supports the required level of thinking and performance?
- What support is available after formal learning?

### 7. Define evidence and assessment

- What evidence will demonstrate performance to the required standard?
- Is a knowledge check, scenario, simulation, work sample, observation, or portfolio appropriate?
- Who will review or validate the evidence?
- What makes the assessment fair, reliable, and relevant?

### 8. Plan evaluation and improvement

- What workforce or business measure should improve?
- How will the organization monitor capability development over time?
- What feedback will update the learning path, performance standard, or assessment?

## Copy-ready alignment prompt

```text
Act as a technical enablement and workforce-development partner.

Help me create an initial capability-alignment outline using the information below. Use clear, standard workforce language. Where information is missing, identify the gap and ask a concise question rather than inventing an answer.

Context:
- Technical or business domain:
- Expected performance outcome:
- Role or team accountable:
- Critical task:
- Technology, system, or workflow involved:
- Relevant NIST Cybersecurity Framework outcome, if applicable:
- Relevant NICE Framework role, task, knowledge, skill, or competency area, if applicable:
- Performance standard:
- Available evidence or assessment method:
- Intended audience:
- Constraints or risks:

Create:
1. A capability statement.
2. A role-and-task alignment summary.
3. Required knowledge and skills.
4. A recommended learning-path sequence.
5. A performance-assessment approach.
6. Evidence of proficiency.
7. Key gaps and questions for stakeholder validation.
```
